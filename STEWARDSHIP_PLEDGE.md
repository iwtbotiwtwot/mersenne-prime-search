# SAM Public Stewardship Pledge

The SAM Research Project makes the following public promise for work released
through this repository.

If an award, prize, or comparable financial recognition is received because
of a result developed or published here, the project will treat that money as
a stewardship responsibility rather than a private windfall. After applicable
taxes, direct validation and legal costs, and reasonable household stability,
the substantial majority will be directed toward public-benefit purposes,
including independent research, education, open technical infrastructure,
humanitarian support, and other carefully documented charitable use.

Exact allocation will depend on the amount, applicable law, and circumstances
at the time. The governing commitments are transparency, restraint, public
benefit, and preservation of the research record.

This pledge is a promise of the SAM Research Project. It is not a condition on
users of the code or documents, and it does not modify the repository license.
